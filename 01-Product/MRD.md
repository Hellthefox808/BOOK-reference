# Market Requirements Document (MRD)

## 1. Market Opportunity & Competitive Landscape
The web-based reading ecosystem is dominated by Kindle Web Reader, Google Books, and open-source readers. However, existing solutions often suffer from outdated web interfaces, rigid DRM wrappers, or lack of cross-device customization.

### Competitive Matrix
| Feature | Kindle Web | Google Books | ReaderPub | BookVerse |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| EPUB & PDF Support | Limited | Yes | Yes | **Native Hybrid Engine** |
| Modern UX / Clean UI | Moderate | Dated | Average | **State-of-the-Art** |
| AI Integration (Summaries/Q&A) | No | No | No | **Planned (Roadmap)** |
| PWA / Offline Caching | Partial | No | Partial | **Full Support** |
| Social & Annotation Sharing | No | No | Limited | **Planned** |

---

## 2. Target Market Demographics
- **Digital Natives (Ages 18-35)**: Expect dark mode, sleek typography, and cloud sync.
- **Academic & Educational (Students/Researchers)**: Require PDF rendering, exact page referencing, highlights, and note taking.
- **Global Readers**: Require lightweight, low-bandwidth browser reading capabilities.
