# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP Core Reader (Q1 2026)
- JWT & NextAuth / Auth.js (Email/Password, Google OAuth).
- Book Discovery: Categories, Search, Trending, Recently Added.
- Custom Web Reader Engine (EPUB via `epubjs`, PDF via `pdfjs-dist`).
- Reading Customization (Font size, font family, line spacing, themes).
- Bookmarks & Reading Progress Cloud Sync.
- Admin Dashboard for Book Uploads & Management.

## Phase 2: Engagement & Social Features (Q2 2026)
- Text Highlighting & Sticky Notes.
- Text-to-Speech (TTS) integration.
- Reading Statistics, Badges & Streaks.
- User Reviews, Ratings, and Community Discussions.
- Offline Caching via Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Phase 3: AI Intelligence & Publisher Ecosystem (Q3-Q4 2026)
- AI Chapter Summaries & Instant Q&A.
- Audiobook sync with EPUB text.
- Author & Publisher Analytics Dashboard.
- DRM protection layer & digital rights licensing.
- Native Mobile Companion Apps (React Native / Expo).
