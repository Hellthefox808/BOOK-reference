# Product Vision Document

## Core Vision
To create the world's most elegant, accessible, and intelligent web-based reading platform—empowering readers everywhere to discover knowledge without software installation boundaries.

---

## Strategic Pillars
1. **Uncompromised Reader Experience**: Typography-first, ultra-responsive engine supporting light, dark, and sepia environments with zero layout shift.
2. **Universal Accessibility**: Native PWA capabilities enabling offline reading on any device regardless of operating system.
3. **Intelligent Reading Assistant**: Leveraging AI to provide instant chapter summaries, contextual dictionary lookups, and AI-driven Q&A.
4. **Creator & Publisher Empowerment**: Secure distribution, granular access controls, and transparent reader analytics.
